# DigitalMe identity, contributor credit and estate rights

Separate **identity**, **contribution credit**, **financial entitlement**, **estate access** and **legal property ownership**. They are not synonyms.

Illustrative contributor-credit inputs from the originating planning text: host/mapping contributor 120, installer 80. These are **examples only**; no credit minted, assigned to an actual person or claim paid.

Credit event must include: `contributor_principal_ref`, `work_ref`, `issuer`, `proof_ref`, `approved_quantity`, `unit`, `policy_version`, `dispute_state`, `evidence_ref`. Warden policy must approve issuance; River retains chain of custody.

Estate rights transition: verified registered title/instrument and applicable law → authenticated principal → scope-specific Warden access grant → DigitalMe binding → River record. An ARK closing event or hash alone is never real property conveyance. Revoke access independently of legal title when the grant expires.
